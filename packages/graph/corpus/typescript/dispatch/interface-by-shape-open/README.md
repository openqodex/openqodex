Two calls through interfaces whose shape the graph cannot read whole. `Cached` has `get` but not `put`, and it extends `LRUCache` from a package outside the repository, which may supply `put`, so it may be a `Store`. `Remote` extends `Api` from a package, so its members are not all known, and `Fetcher`, which has `fetch`, may or may not have the rest. The change edits `Cached.get` and `Fetcher.fetch`. A graph that lists classes by their shape must not list either one as a match it cannot prove, and must not drop the `unsupported-rule` gap at either call, since it is what tells the reviewer that both methods may be reached and their caller lists are a floor.
